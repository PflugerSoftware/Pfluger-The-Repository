# R&B Hype Video Shotlist

**Format:** Square 1:1 (final export 1440x1440)
**Capture:** Record at 4K 16:9 (3840x2160), crop to 1:1 in post
**Total runtime:** ~34 seconds
**Pacing:** Fast cuts, music-driven, no voiceover
**Approach:** Typography carries the story. Small/light weight for setup, large/bold weight for the punchline.
**Why square:** plays native on LinkedIn feed + IG feed, fits cleanly in a presentation slide, works on mobile and desktop without re-export. One master, post everywhere.

## Production notes

- **Capture resolution:** 4K horizontal (3840x2160) for crop headroom
- **Final export:** 1:1 square at 1440x1440 (or 1080x1080 if you need smaller files)
- **Recording tool:** QuickTime for clean Mac recording, or Cmd+Shift+5 for higher framerate
- **Browser setup:** zoom the page to ~125% before recording so UI fills the frame; hide bookmarks bar; full-screen the browser window
- **Mapbox shots:** record on a fresh page load to catch the fly-in animation
- **Ezra shots:** pre-warm the prompt once before recording so streamed responses feel snappy
- **Confidential projects:** Lee College and WCJC are flagged confidential. Record those logged-out only if you want them visible in the public version. Logged-in shows everything.

## Square crop workflow

1. Capture everything 4K horizontal — gives you headroom and lets you re-frame any shot in post
2. In your editor, set the canvas to 1:1 (1440x1440)
3. Per shot, scale and position the captured clip so the action sits centered in the square
4. For shots where critical content lives at the edges (campus sidebar, chat panel side), use a slow pan or a quick zoom to bring it into the square frame
5. Export once. Post the same file to LinkedIn, IG feed, and embed in the presentation slide

## Typography direction

- **Small/light:** SF Pro Display Light or Regular, 60-80px, white or light gray. Sets up the punchline.
- **Large/bold:** SF Pro Display Bold or Black, 140-200px, white. Drops the punchline.
- **Orange transition (Shot 9):** Pfluger Orange `#F2A900` on black background. Same large/bold weight.
- **Stack vertically** with the small line above the large line. Center-aligned. Tight line-height.
- **Animation:** small line fades in first, large line punches in 200-300ms later with a quick scale or weight shift.

## Sound design (no VO)

- **Music:** ambient-driving build. Search "tech showcase," "documentary uplifting," or "minimal piano build" on Epidemic Sound. The drop should land on Shot 9 (the orange "ya got an idea?" pivot) or Shot 14.
- **SFX layers:**
  - Whoosh on every cut between sections
  - Soft click on pin drops (Shots 3, 6)
  - UI tick on chat messages appearing (Shots 7, 8, 12)
  - Bright ding or chime on greenlight (Shot 11)
  - Bass thump on Shots 9 and 14 to land the pattern interrupts
